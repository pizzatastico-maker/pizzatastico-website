PIZZATASTICO DOUGH ENGINE (PDE)

Coupled Dough Biochemistry in Real Time

The Pizzatastico Dough Engine (PDE) links published cereal-chemistry and yeast-kinetics models into a closed system of differential equations across your entire fermentation schedule.

MODEL ARCHITECTURE

Dynamic Computation Across Temperature Phases

During dough fermentation, yeast metabolism, enzymatic gluten relaxation, starch cleavage by amylases, and organic acid formation occur simultaneously—yet each responds differently to transitions between room and cold proofing.

The Pizzatastico Dough Engine (PDE) couples these biochemical processes in a time-resolved differential equation system, accounting for the thermal cooling and warming inertia of the dough mass as well as custom flour blends (Tipo 00, Tipo 0, Tipo 1).

SCIENTIFIC FOUNDATION

Published Literature in the PDE Core

Every term in the engine is grounded in established cereal chemistry and microbiology:

📚 Wieser (2007) pH- & temperature-dependent proteolysis

1. Endogenous Aspartic Proteases & Gluten Maturation

Endogenous flour aspartic proteases relax the gluten network as a function of pH and temperature. The PDE models structural maturation across bulk and final proof independently of gas production.

🌡️ Sharpe-DeMichele / Schoolfield Thermodynamic S. cerevisiae kinetics

2. Cardinal Temperature & Membrane-Lipid Transition

Biophysical temperature model for Saccharomyces cerevisiae covering the full range from cold membrane-lipid phase transition in the fridge to the cardinal optimum.

💧 Raoult / Norrish / Donnan Free water · NaCl Donnan binding · Osmotic pressure

3. Water Activity & Osmosis

Accounts for biopolymer-bound water in flour, electrostatic Donnan ion binding of NaCl to the gluten network, and osmotic effects on yeast kinetics.

⚖️ Embden-Meyerhof-Parnas + Neuberg Amylase replenishment & fermentation balance

4. Closed Substrate & Hexose Stoichiometry

Closed mass balance from enzymatic cleavage of damaged starch by α/β-amylases through the Embden-Meyerhof-Parnas glycolytic pathway and Neuberg fermentation byproducts.
