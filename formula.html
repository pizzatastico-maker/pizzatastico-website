1. CORE EQUATIONS

Baker's Percentages & Ingredient Scaling

Every serious dough formula starts with Baker's Percentages: total flour weight M is set to 100%, and water (H), salt (S), oil/fat (F), and yeast (Y) are expressed relative to flour. Starting from your target total dough weight T = Balls × Ball Weight × (1 + Bowl Loss), flour mass is solved inversely.

Closed Ingredient Scaling Formulas

T = N_balls × m_ball × (1 + Loss%)
M_flour = T / (1 + H% + S% + F% + Y%)
Water = M × H% · Salt = M × S% · Fat = M × F%

Worked Example (4 × 250 g = 1,000 g, 65% Hydration, 2.8% Salt)

With trace yeast (< 0.5%) omitted from total mass: M_flour = 1,000 g / (1 + 0.65 + 0.028) = 1,000 / 1.678 ≈ 596 g flour. Water = 595.9 g × 0.65 ≈ 387 g. Salt = 595.9 g × 0.028 ≈ 16.7 g. Exact yeast varies from 0.15 g to 3.50 g depending on time, room/fridge temperature, dough mass, and flour blend.

Fresh Yeast (CY) vs. Dry Yeast (IDY / ADY) & Sourdough

Simple formulas convert Fresh Yeast (CY = 100%) using fixed rules of thumb (IDY ≈ 0.33×, ADY ≈ 0.40–0.45×). Pizzatastico goes further: PDE 5.3.0 simulates rehydration survival, glutathione (GSH) softening, plus Licoli and Lievito Madre Type-I sourdough down to 0.01g.

2. WHY 0D CURVES BREAK DOWN

Physical Limits of Tables & Generic 0D Fixed-Q10 Models

Classic 0D point models with a fixed Q10 = 2.0 deliver strong guidance for warm direct doughs. In real home kitchens with cold fridges, varying dough masses, and custom flours, four physical effects go beyond single-point equations:

1 · THERMAL INERTIA

250 g Ball vs. 2 kg Bulk Block in the Fridge

0D point models assume a warm 23 °C dough reaches 4–7 °C instantly. In reality, self-insulating CO2 foam and fermentation heat keep the core of a 1–2.5 kg bulk block warm for hours — causing 25–45% summer overproofing if yeast is not adjusted across radial shells.

2 · 11.8× COLD BIAS

Cryokinetics Below 9 °C (Fixed Q10 = 2.0 Error)

A fixed Q10 = 2.0 curve predicts that 5 °C fridge yeast runs at 17.7% of its 30 °C rate. Measured laboratory data shows only ~1.5% due to membrane lipid stiffening and ergosterol arrest — an 11.8× overestimate in naive exponential formulas.

3 · OSMOSIS & FLOUR MATRIX
