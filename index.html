Frequently Asked Questions

Does Pizzatastico use lookup tables or real mathematical formulas?

Pizzatastico uses zero lookup tables. Building on the foundation of Baker's Percentages and classic 0D fermentation curves, Pizzatastico solves a spatially resolved 5-shell PDE (PDE 5.3.0) modeling core cooling lag, 4-5 °C cryokinetics, salt/sugar water activity, and flour blends locally offline.

How does the Pizzatastico dough calculator work?

Pizzatastico uses zero static lookup tables. It solves a real 1D-radial 5-shell continuum physics model (PDE 5.3.0) directly on your device in native C++20, computing flour, water, salt, and yeast or sourdough down to 0.01g based on ambient/fridge temperature, dough mass, flour blend protein, salt/sugar osmosis, and your target bake time.

Which dough methods does Pizzatastico support (Direct Dough, Poolish, Biga, Sourdough)?

Pizzatastico calculates Direct Dough (pure room temperature or combined refrigerator cold maturation), 2-stage preferments (Poolish and Biga), and Type-I sourdough (liquid Licoli and stiff Lievito Madre). Using the 2-stage PDE 5.3.0 cascade, the matured preferment or starter feeds mass- and hydration-continuously into the main dough.

How does the calendar export for my baking schedule work?

With one tap, Pizzatastico syncs your complete schedule (kneading, ball shaping, oven preheating) directly into your device calendar (Google Calendar or Apple Calendar) with reminder alerts. Alternatively, you can export a universal .ics file to share via WhatsApp or email.

What notifications and timers does the app provide?

The app reliably alerts you via push notifications for every milestone: with a configurable pre-flight alarm before kneading starts, on-time alerts at phase transitions (e.g. when bulk fermentation ends and balls must be shaped), and an ongoing live countdown chronometer in your notification drawer.

How are fresh yeast and dry yeast converted?

Pizzatastico supports Fresh Cake Yeast (CY), Instant Dry Yeast (IDY), Active Dry Yeast (ADY), as well as Licoli and Lievito Madre sourdough down to 0.01g. Instead of crude fixed multipliers, PDE 5.3.0 simulates dry-yeast rehydration survival, glutathione (GSH) dough relaxation, and sourdough microbial symbiosis.

What hydration and flour are recommended for beginners?

For beginners using a domestic home oven, we recommend 58% to 62% hydration and an authentic Tipo 00 flour with 11.5% to 12.5% protein. For high-temperature pizza ovens, 62% to 68% with stronger flour (12.5% to 13.5% protein) delivers optimal airy results.
